## Description
This tech is caused by holding holding backwards for 1 or more ticks after an [[extendedslideinstantpounce]] or by doing it on the 17th tick of a slide, causing you get launched backwards instead.

## Notes
This is considered a failure to do an [[extendedslideinstantpounce|eipounce]], [[extendedslideinstantjump|eijump]] or [[extendedslideinstanthop|eihop]], as is a common way to fail with an undesirable movement direction sometimes leading to flying off the map.
