## Description
A variant of a [[flip]] with a fixed arc, produced by pressing backwards and jump during frames 14 through 16 of a [[slide]]. It is always possible to [[fliproll]] from this move.

## Notes


## Performed from
[[slide]]

## Allows for
[[slidethrow]], [[downthrow]]

## Chains to
[[fliproll]]
