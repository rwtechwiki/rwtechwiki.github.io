## Description
If you perform a [[crawlturncancel]], there is a 4-tick (begins on the 5th tick from initial backwards input) window after leaving the ground where slugcat is able to jump mid-air.

## Notes
This gives slugcat a maximum of 63.6 units / ~3 extra tile of jump height, becoming able to [[ledgepullup]] 5-tile walls. The optimal timing is pressing Back for 4 ticks with an up on the second tick which you can continue to hold but don't need to as it doesn't change anything from that point onward, then hold nothing for 3 ticks then jump for at least 7 ticks.
